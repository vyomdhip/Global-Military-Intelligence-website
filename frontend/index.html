Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Global Military Intelligence ("GMI", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Global Military Intelligence website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using our Service, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Registration Data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Username — a unique identifier you choose (minimum 3 characters)
  • Email address — used for account identification
  • Password — stored in hashed form using bcrypt (12 salt rounds); we never store or have access to your plaintext password
  • Display name — optional, defaults to your username

Automatically Recorded Data

Our system automatically records the following timestamps:

  • Account creation date (createdAt)
  • Last profile update (updatedAt)
  • Last login date — updated each time you sign in

We do not store IP addresses in our database, track geolocation, use browser fingerprinting, or run usage analytics. No third-party analytics or tracking scripts are embedded in the Service. Standard network-level requests may temporarily be processed by the hosting server solely for security and DDoS mitigation.

3. Authentication & Security

How Authentication Works

  • Authentication is handled via JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with a server-side secret
  • Tokens are issued at login and registration, and expire after 7 days
  • Tokens contain your user ID, username, and role ("user" or "admin") — no sensitive data such as your email or password is included in the token
  • Passwords are hashed using bcryptjs with a work factor of 12 before storage — we cannot retrieve or view your original password

Password Changes

You can change your password through the profile settings. The system verifies your current password before accepting any change. New passwords must be at least 6 characters and differ from the current password.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This Service does not use browser cookies.

We use browser localStorage to store two items on your device:

  • gmi_token — your JWT authentication token (used to keep you logged in)
  • gmi_user — a JSON object containing your user ID, username, email, role, and display name (used to render the UI without additional API calls)

Both items are removed from your browser when you log out. You can also manually clear them at any time via your browser's developer tools or settings.

5. Watchlist Data

Authenticated users can follow nations or conflicts by adding them to a personal watchlist. Each watchlist entry stores:

  • Your user ID (linking the entry to your account)
  • The target type ("nation" or "conflict")
  • The target identifier (e.g. country key)
  • Timestamp of when the item was added

Watchlist data is private to your account and is not shared with other users or third parties. You can remove any item from your watchlist at any time.

6. Analyst Reports

Authenticated users can create analyst reports (notebooks). Each report stores:

  • Your user ID (as the author)
  • Report title (up to 200 characters) and text content
  • Embedded data widgets (comparison charts, fleet distributions, force tables) with their configuration
  • A public/private flag — you choose whether to share reports in the Community Hub
  • Creation and last-updated timestamps

Private reports are visible only to you and site administrators. Public reports are visible to all visitors and display your display name as the author.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage — plaintext passwords are never persisted
  • API endpoints that access personal data (profile, watchlist, reports) require a valid JWT
  • Administrative endpoints are additionally restricted to accounts with the "admin" role
  • The database is hosted on MongoDB Atlas, which provides encryption at rest and in transit

While we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services:

  • MongoDB Atlas — cloud database hosting for user accounts, watchlists, and reports
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter and Rajdhani typefaces; Google may collect limited usage data per their privacy policy
  • Leaflet / OpenStreetMap — provides the interactive map; map tile requests are sent to OpenStreetMap tile servers
  • CDNJS (Cloudflare) — serves the GSAP animation library
  • RSS News Feeds — the server fetches publicly available RSS feeds from defense news outlets (USNI News, Naval News, UK Defence Journal, LiveFist Defence, Indian Defence Research Wing, Indian Defence News) to display news articles; no user data is sent to these sources

We do not integrate any advertising networks, social media trackers, or third-party analytics services.

9. Data Retention

Your account data, watchlist entries, and analyst reports are retained for as long as your account exists. Server-side news feed caches are temporary and are refreshed approximately every 10 minutes.

If you wish to have your account and all associated data deleted, please contact us using the details below.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

  • Access — view the personal data we hold about you (available through the Profile page)
  • Rectification — update your display name or change your password through the Profile settings
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us (processed within 30 days)
  • Portability — request a copy of your data in a structured format
  • Withdrawal of consent — you can stop using the Service at any time; log out to remove locally stored data, and contact us to request account deletion

11. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for general audiences and defense intelligence research. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 where required by applicable law).

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will promptly delete such records.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries, data requests, or account deletion, please contact us:
